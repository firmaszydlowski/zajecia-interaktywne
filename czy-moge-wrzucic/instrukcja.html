Cel

Zanim uczeń coś opublikuje, wie, co może wrzucić, czego nie może i gdzie szukać legalnych zdjęć i muzyki. Kiedy prawo nie daje pewnej odpowiedzi, wie, że trzeba zapytać opiekuna.

23. Legal photos and music

Slide

You say: There is a full list with licence terms on the phones. It stays with you after the class. For you: ShareAlike requires the same licence for edited versions. Besides the licence, other rights may apply: trademarks, moral rights, the image of people in the photo. FMA treats music in a video as a derivative work, and YouTube Content ID may flag a video even with a compliant licence.

  • Wikimedia Commons: Every file has its own licence: check it on the file page. Usually you must give the author, the name of the licence with a link, and the source. With a ShareAlike licence, an edited version must have the same licence. Also check that the photo has no recognisable people or logos.
  • Pixabay: You may use and edit them for free. Naming the author is not required, but welcome. You may not sell unchanged copies or use the photos to mislead anyone. You also may not use recognisable people in an immoral or illegal way. Content with recognisable logos may not be used commercially. Check music separately, in the terms next to the track.
  • Unsplash: You may download and use them for free, also commercially, without asking the author. Naming the author (“Photo by … on Unsplash”) is not required, but welcome. You may not sell photos without a significant change or copy the site. Unsplash's rules say nothing about people in photos and about logos, so judge such photos separately.
  • Freesound: Sounds have CC0, CC BY (give the author, the name of the sound, the link and the licence) or CC BY-NC (give the author; no commercial use). A school website with no ads or profit is usually non-commercial. Not sure? Choose CC0 or CC BY.
  • Free Music Archive: Every track has its own licence (BY, BY-SA, BY-NC, BY-ND, CC0). Check it on the left side of the track card. According to FMA, music under a video is an edited version of the track, so an ND licence does not allow it. NC rules out commercial use, and ads or promoting a product can make the use commercial. Some tracks have YouTube Content ID, so the video may get flagged even with a proper licence.
